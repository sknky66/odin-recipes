Chocolate Mousse Recipe

Description

Chocolate mousse is a timeless classic among French desserts.

Ingredients

  • 200g dark Chocolate (50% to 70% cocoa)
  • 6 ultra-fresh eggs
  • 1 pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Melt the chocolate in a double boiler (bain-marie) or in the microwave at low power.
    Let it cool until warm.
  2. Separate the egg whites from the yolks.
  3. Stir the yolks one by one into the melted chocolate, mixing vigorously.
  4. Whip the egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff peaks form.
  5. Gently fold 1/3 of the whites into the chocolate mixture to loosen it, then fold in the rest
    gently using a spatula, lifting from the bottom to keep the airiness.
  6. Pour into ramekins and refrigerate for at least 3 hours (ideally overnight)
