Tiramisu Recipe

Description

Tiramisu is the iconic Italian dessert, famous for its mascarpone cream and coffee-soaked ladyfingers.

Ingredients (for 6 serves)

  • 250g mascarpone cheese
  • 3 ultra-fresh eggs
  • 80g granulated sugar
  • 1 packet vanilla sugar
  • 24 ladyfingers
  • 25cl strong black coffee
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • (Optional) 2 tbsp Amaretto or Marsala wine added to coffee

Instructions

  1. Separate the egg whites from the yolks.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the granulated sugar and vanilla sugar until the mixture becomes
    pale and frothy
  3. Add the mascarpone to the yolk mixture, then whisk vigorously until smooth and
    lump-free.
  4. Whip the egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff peaks form.
  5. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the mascarpone cream using a spatula, lifting
    from the bottom so they don't deflate.
  6. Quickly dip the ladyfingers into the cooled coffee (do not soak them for too long).
  7. Assemble in a baking dish or individual glasses:
    • A first layer of soaked ladyfingers.
    • A layer of mascarpone cream.
    • Repeat the process (finish with a cream layer).
  8. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (ideally 12 to 24 hours for the best texture).
  9. Dust with cocoa powder just before serving.
